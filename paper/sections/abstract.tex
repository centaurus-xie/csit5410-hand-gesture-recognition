Hand landmarks provide a compact representation for recognizing number
gestures from an ordinary webcam. We compare normalized coordinates, explicit
hand geometry, and their combination using radial-basis support vector machines
and small multilayer perceptrons. The task covers ten project-defined digit
labels and a class of non-target poses. A pretrained hand detector retained
1,825 of 2,260 candidate images, including augmented copies. On 249 held-out
original photographs with valid landmarks, the combined-feature perceptron
classified 246 correctly (98.8\% accuracy, 96.1\% macro-$F_1$). The split groups
images by original photograph and leaves signer identity uncontrolled. We
export the perceptron to a browser demonstration that converts confirmed digit
predictions into spoken numbers or musical notes. The results quantify
classification after landmark extraction and motivate independent evaluation of
the full webcam pipeline.
