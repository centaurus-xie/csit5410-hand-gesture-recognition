\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A compact landmark representation supports classification and browser
deployment for the project's static digit vocabulary. The combined MLP
achieves 98.8\% accuracy on the retained test originals, while uneven
landmark yield and the low sample counts for three digits limit conclusions
about the full system. The effect of adding geometry depends on the
classifier and the metric used. A follow-up webcam study should record
participants absent from training and count detection failures, rejected
predictions, correct outputs, and incorrect outputs over all attempts.
Dynamic signs provide a second test of the representation, where manually
segmented video clips can measure the contribution of wrist motion.
